## Change requests this run raises

Recorded here rather than acted on, because they change published design
numbers and WORKING_DISCIPLINE §6 makes that a decision rather than an edit.

1. **The average ticket and the menu prices disagree.** ECONOMY_DESIGN §3 builds
   the envelope on a ₡4.50 average ticket; §4 publishes three Stage 1 prices that
   average ₡3.33 under the uniform item choice the simulation implements. Phase 12
   scaled the three prices *and* their ingredient costs by 1.35 to close it with
   every margin unchanged. When the fourteen-item menu exists and choice is
   weighted by the `appealTags` §4 already carries, they should go back to
   ₡3 / ₡5 / ₡2 and the average should arrive from the mix.
2. **Stages 2 to 4 have no content.** Three of fourteen menu items and five of the
   upgrade tree exist. Three assertions cannot be evaluated until they do.
3. **An idle player cannot progress through Stage 1.** §13 asks every policy to
   reach Stage 2 inside 10–22 minutes; §5.1 makes the cook a Stage 2 role, so
   Stage 1 cannot be automated at all. The two cannot both hold. Measured: 85
   minutes for `idle-player` against 23 for an attentive one.
4. **The game lets you evolve into a stage you cannot operate.** A stand holding
   ₡804 may accept a ₡800 transition and open Stage 3 with ₡4 — too little to hire
   the waiter the stage needs. Measured before the policies were taught to keep a
   float: zero income, zero staff and zero customers for the remaining ten hours,
   with no way back. Cash never went negative, so nothing objected.
